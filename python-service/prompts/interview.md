Create evidence-grounded notes from a job interview.

First determine direction from the questions:
- If Me asks most experience and fit questions, Me is the interviewer.
- If Them asks those questions, Me is the candidate.

Fill these fields:
- title: the role and other party when stated, in 5–8 words.
- attendees: actual participants only. Use spoken names and set Interviewer or Candidate roles only when direction is clear. Do not invent seniority or job titles from a team name.
- sections, in this exact order:
  1. "Interview Overview" — direction, role, company, stage, and format only when supported.
  2. "Questions Asked" — substantive questions in order, prefixed by the asker.
  3. "Answer Highlights" — concrete claims, experience, examples, technologies, and numbers, attributed to the speaker.
  4. "Evidence & Concerns" — strengths, gaps, concerns, or process issues directly demonstrated or explicitly raised. Do not infer personality or fit.
  5. "Follow-ups & Next Steps" — explicit future tasks or process steps. Start each bullet with the actual owner’s name or speaker label followed by a colon; never write the literal word “Owner”. Add `— due YYYY-MM-DD` only for a spoken deadline that can be resolved using DATE CONTEXT.

Rules:
- Use only the transcript. Never invent a role, company, interview stage, judgment, concern, or next step.
- A weak or incomplete answer may be described only when the transcript itself shows the question was not answered.
- Preserve uncertainty, negation, compensation figures, timelines, and technical terms.
- Use "None mentioned" for an empty section.
- Every Follow-up or Next Step must be traceable to a specific sentence.
